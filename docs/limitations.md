# Interpretation limits

Both cohorts were explored before this project; no experiment is untouched external confirmation. Small sample sizes, absent verified person identifiers, cohort sampling differences (GSE99511 self-samples versus GSE46306 cervical tissue), HPV/class association in GSE46306 and preprocessing differences limit generalization. Random CV cannot demonstrate transfer to unseen chips. LOCO may be difficult and some chips may contain one class. No chip is excluded to improve results and no causal chip-versus-biology explanation is claimed.

The deposited GSE46306 scale metadata are contradictory despite numerical evidence supporting beta values. The local series-matrix gzip is truncated; its readable metadata header supplies labels and mappings, while the separate complete processed supplement supplies model inputs. Existing reconstruction provenance is inferred from saved files and reference code, not independently recreated from IDATs. Specific historical mask settings cannot be fully established without execution records.

The custom DDPM is small and uses fixed unoptimized settings. With only 100 linear-schedule steps its terminal distribution need not be close to standard normal, potentially harming generation. No duration, schedule, feature-count, seed or threshold search uses test outcomes. Synthetic rows do not increase the number of independent people. Diagnostic similarity does not prove biological validity or privacy. Descriptive repeat/seed standard deviations are not confidence intervals and repeated predictions are not independent replicates.
